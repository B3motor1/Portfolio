Micromouse Autonomous Robot

2025 — 2026
C++ KiCad EasyEDA IMU Embedded Flood-Fill
  • Challenge: Solve an unknown maze fully on-board — no off-board compute, no external localization, and light enough to stay quick through tight turns.
  • Build: Designed a custom PCB in KiCad and EasyEDA that doubles as the robot's structural body, cutting part count and mass, with IMU-based heading correction for straight-line and turn accuracy.
  • Result: C++ firmware runs a flood-fill solver: an exploration pass maps the maze and updates the distance field, then the robot commits to the fastest known route to the center.

Solar Greenhouse Recorder

2025
ESP32-CAM Embedded C++ Python Solar / Battery 3D Printing
  • Challenge: Record weeks of plant growth inside a mini greenhouse with no wall power and no daily human intervention.
  • Build: A solar panel and battery pack feed an ESP32-CAM that wakes on a timed interval to capture a frame and returns to deep sleep, stretching runtime between charge cycles. Mount and enclosure were prototyped to hold a fixed camera angle shot to shot.
  • Result: A Python script compiles the captured stills into timelapse video of the full growth cycle.
