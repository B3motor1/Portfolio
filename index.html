About

I’m an electrical engineering student at the University of Houston who spends most of my free time on hands-on DIY hardware. I like taking an idea all the way from a rough sketch to a finished physical build, handling the custom PCB layouts in KiCad, flashing microcontrollers, and 3D printing the enclosures.

For me, the best way to learn is by jumping on things blind and figuring it out along the way. Whether it’s a small utility board or a multi-part build, I just enjoy turning ideas into functional physical projects.
